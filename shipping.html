Questions

Shipping FAQ

Which countries do you ship to?

We currently ship across all GCC countries: Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes 3–10 business days depending on the destination.

Can delivery take longer?

Occasionally, delivery may take longer due to customs, holidays, or unexpected shipping delays.
